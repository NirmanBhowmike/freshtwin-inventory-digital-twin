MODEL DESIGN

Why joint optimization matters

ONE STORE
01Forecast demand

Each vegetable has separate demand variability, shelf life, supplier lead time and scenario costs. Historic inputs are generated with known latent demand for controlled simulation.

02Constrain every order

All policies share a total purchasing budget and a conservative cold-storage reservation. Quantities are integer; balances cannot go negative or exceed capacity.

03Allocate marginal value

A bounded candidate search evaluates FEFO rollouts and allocates profitable quantities across SKUs. This is a heuristic, not a guaranteed globally optimal solution.

04Compare matched trials

Recorded-sales, corrected-demand and coupled decisions face identical future demand in each trial. Results report cost, service, spoilage, model risk and constraint binding.

Evidence boundaryNamed products and cost inputs are illustrative. The 865 anonymous benchmark records remain separate and are not treated as a real ten-product store.

03 / SCIENTIFIC TRANSPARENCY

Every number has an assumption.

FreshTwin is a reproducible engineering research prototype. Its purpose is to explore inventory decision mechanics and test hypotheses, not to claim verified field performance.

01

Censored-demand estimation

Estimate demand suppressed by observed stockout hours using a bounded availability adjustment and uncensored-sales anchor. This heuristic is not a statistically identified recovery model.

HEURISTIC · TRANSPARENT
02

Perishable stock-flow dynamics

Track opening stock, receipts, realized sales, expired units, ending stock and outstanding purchase orders. Fulfillment follows first-expiring-first-out (FEFO) allocation.

DISCRETE TIME · AGE-AWARE
03

Cost-aligned stochastic order search

Choose replenishment quantities using scenario-based FEFO rollouts and the same cost components reported after evaluation, including opening inventory assets and remaining stock and pipeline credits at cost.

SAMPLE AVERAGE · APPROXIMATE
04

Reproducible evaluation

Use seeded pseudo-random demand paths, common scenarios across policies, repeated trials and explicitly defined CVaR tail risk to compare performance.

MODEL FORMULATION · SIMULATION COST ACCOUNTING

What exactly is being minimized?

PER SKU

For every evaluation horizon, the model compares three replenishment policies using the same generated demand scenarios. Order quantities are nonnegative integers. The freshness-aware policy searches a bounded set of candidate quantities; it is not a globally optimal multi-period solution.

Daily stock conservationClosing = Opening + Receipts − Sales − ExpiredFEFO serves the units with the shortest remaining shelf life first. Demand that cannot be filled is lost.
Reported net scenario costCost = c × (Opening + Purchased − Usable closing − Paid incoming) + Holding + Disposal + Shortage penaltiesHere c is assumed purchase cost per unit. Remaining usable stock and outstanding paid orders retain their cost-basis asset value, avoiding end-of-horizon order-expense distortions.
Supply and shelf-life restrictionsOrder ≥ 0, integer; arrival = order day + lead time + 1Daily batch ages decrease, expired units leave inventory, and no item may be sold after expiry. All operating quantities are nonnegative.
Statistical interpretationPaired simulation paths · mean ± approximate 95% CI · sample CVaR90These describe outcomes conditional on scenario assumptions. They do not establish actual retailer savings or identify genuine unobserved demand.
DATA PROVENANCE

Data sources and import

BENCHMARK READY

The default dataset is FreshRetailNet-50K by Dingdong-Inc (CC BY 4.0). Product and category IDs are anonymized; sales values are globally normalized, not real unit counts. The application includes a representative 90-day store history for each product. Use the catalog on the Overview page to select one. You may alternatively import an eight-day-or-longer CSV of physical-unit sales and unavailable hours. CSV stays in your browser session. Imported values do not become benchmark observations.

Scenario inventory is constructed by converting the normalized sales index to illustrative scenario units using a documented multiplier of 10.
