03 / SCIENTIFIC TRANSPARENCY

Every number has an assumption.

FreshTwin is a reproducible engineering research prototype. Its purpose is to explore inventory decision mechanics and test hypotheses, not to claim verified field performance.

01

Censored-demand estimation

Estimate demand suppressed by observed stockout hours using a bounded availability adjustment and uncensored-sales anchor. This heuristic is not a statistically identified recovery model.

HEURISTIC · TRANSPARENT
02

Perishable stock-flow dynamics

Track opening stock, receipts, realized sales, expired units, ending stock and pipeline orders. Fulfillment follows first-expiring-first-out (FEFO) allocation.

DISCRETE TIME · AGE-AWARE
03

Stochastic order search

Select replenishment quantities from a bounded integer grid by minimizing estimated shortage, holding, spoilage and purchase costs across simulated scenarios.

SAMPLE AVERAGE · APPROXIMATE
04

Reproducible evaluation

Use seeded pseudo-random demand paths, common scenarios across policies, repeated trials and explicitly defined CVaR tail risk to compare performance.
